The Spark

From Bengaluru to the World

We started in Bengaluru after a 2-month validation trip across Europe. Facing the complexity of multiple visa requirements, and separate bookings, we realized the travel space was ripe for smart flight technology.

Our Core Philosophy

We believe travel should be fluid. Traditional search engines are completely blind to passport capabilities and existing visa permits, recommending direct and expensive point-to-point routes.

By integrating directly with industry leaders like TBO.COM for flight ticketing and VISA2FLY for electronic visa filings, our smart matching engine automatically maps compliant visa-free routing options instantly.
