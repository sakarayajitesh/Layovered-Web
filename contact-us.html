What is Layovered?

Layovered is an AI-powered travel app that helps you find cheap international flights, discover visa-free countries by passport, and plan smarter trips. Whether you’re an Indian passport holder, US traveler, or global explorer, Layovered makes travel simpler and more affordable.

How Does Layovered Find Cheaper Flights Than Other Search Engines?

The secret is our proprietary "Visa-Aware AI." Traditional flight search engines are "visa-unaware"—they don't consider the unique travel power of your passport and existing visas. Layovered is different. Our AI scans flight routes using layovers in countries you can legally enter, even for a short transit. This strategy unlocks thousands of new flight combinations and hidden routes, leading to significant savings on airfare. Users can find flight deals up to 50% cheaper than on mainstream booking sites.

What Can I Do With the Layovered App Besides Finding Flights?

No, finding affordable flights is just one part of our comprehensive travel platform. Layovered is your all-in-one tool for smarter international travel. Our main features include:

  • Instant Visa Checker: Get a complete list of visa-free countries, e-visa options, and visa-on-arrival policies based on your passport and current visas.
  • Visa-Aware Flight Search: Discover the cheapest, visa-compliant flight paths that other search engines miss, turning your passport into a money-saving tool.
  • AI Travel Assistant: Simply chat with our AI in plain English to plan complex trips. Get instant, bookable itineraries without messing with dozens of filters.
How Do I Find Visa-Free Countries for My Passport?

It's simple. Just select your passport nationality in the Layovered app. Our AI instantly analyzes complex visa rules from hundreds of official government sources to show you every country you can visit. You'll see a clear, updated list of all your travel options, including:

  • Visa-Free destinations
  • Countries offering Visa-on-Arrival
  • Countries with simple e-Visa processes
How Can My Existing Visas (e.g., US, UK, Schengen) Help Me Save Money?

Your existing visas are powerful money-saving tools. Many countries offer visa-waiver programs if you hold a valid visa from the US, UK, Schengen Area, Canada, and others. In the Layovered app, simply add your existing visas. Our AI then does two things:

  • Unlocks New Destinations: It instantly adds all the new countries you can now access to your visa-free list.
  • Finds Cheaper Flights: More importantly, it uses these new countries as potential layover hubs, drastically increasing the number of available flight routes and uncovering cheaper, visa-compliant itineraries you won't find anywhere else.

How Does the Layovered AI Chat Assistant Work?

Our AI Chat feature works like your personal travel agent. Instead of struggling with confusing filters, you can just type what you want in natural language. Our AI chatbot is designed to understand complex, multi-constraint travel queries. For example, you could ask:

  • "Find me the cheapest flight to a visa-free country with warm beaches in December."
  • "A 10-day trip to Southeast Asia, starting from my city, budget of ₹1200."

The AI assistant instantly processes your request and provides the best bookable itineraries that match your exact needs, saving you hours of research.
