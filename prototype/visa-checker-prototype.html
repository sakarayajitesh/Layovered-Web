How far can an Indian passport take you?

An Indian passport gives visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to a meaningful list of destinations across Asia, Africa, and the Caribbean. The list grows considerably once you hold a valid US, Schengen, UK, Canada, or UAE visa — many countries that normally require a visa will admit you on the strength of one you already have.

Use the checker above to see your real list with stay durations and indicative costs. Open any destination in the Layovered app for the exact documents required and the official application link.

Does a US visa let me enter other countries visa-free?

Yes — several countries (e.g. Mexico, Turkey, Costa Rica, Panama, Colombia) admit Indian passport holders who hold a valid US visa. The checker marks these with an “unlocked” tag.

Is visa-on-arrival the same as visa-free?

No. Visa-free means no visa at all; visa-on-arrival means you obtain it at the airport, sometimes for a fee. Both are shown above with the duration and cost.

Where do I see the documents and apply links?

Those open in the Layovered app, alongside step-by-step guidance and the cheapest routing for your trip.
